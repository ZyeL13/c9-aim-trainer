      How it scores

      • Base 100 per hit, plus a speed bonus (up to +100 for the fastest flicks).
      • Combo bonus: +5 per hit in your current streak, capped at +100.
      • Miss (clicking empty space or letting a target fade) resets the combo to zero.
      • Targets shrink as the round goes on — stay sharp.
      • Clutch Challenge: targets move; gold ★ targets are worth +150, red ✕ targets cost −100 and break your combo. Final 15 seconds: all points ×2.
      • Only 60s rounds count toward the leaderboard — 30s and 90s are practice.
